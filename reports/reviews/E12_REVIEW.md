Status: **COMPLETE; FREEZE PERMITTED WITH NEGATIVE EXPECTATION**.

On 300 non-protected qualification worlds, AWARE selected independent DiD in
298 worlds and abstained twice: overall 0.67%, clean 0%, coverage 0.913. Mean
normalized decision regret was 0.43441 versus 0.43474 for fixed DiD—a negligible
development difference. The selector has not demonstrated meaningful selection
gain and complexity receives no credit.

Fresh review attempted to falsify estimand mapping, truth/time leakage,
treatment unit, donor contamination, interval labels, design mismatch,
post-selection claims, multiplicity, denominator completeness, and package
identity. Blocking findings fixed before this review were missing-data loss
normalization, overly strict gates, and uncalibrated independent intervals.
Remaining nonblocking threats are shared diagnostics, low selection diversity,
weak compound-OOD coverage, and approximate independent estimators. The frozen
test may fail H2 or coverage; thresholds will not be weakened after E14.
