# Data-Quality Stress Study

Official GeoX quality checks executed for all 18 perturbation cells: {'PASS': 18}. A separate official design execution followed each check: 10 designs were available and 8 were gated or failed validation/design requirements. Thus a quality-check pass is not treated as proof that a design can be produced. Full failure text is retained for all 8 unavailable designs. Warnings and structured metrics are retained verbatim in canonical evidence. Data-order shuffling did not itself trigger a hard error; duplicate records were aggregated with warnings, and outliers/zero-response geos were identified. The separate proxy rows are explicitly labeled and are not attributed to GeoX.
