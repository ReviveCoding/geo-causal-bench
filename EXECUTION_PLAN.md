# Geo-Causal Measurement & Econometrics Benchmark ExecPlan

This living plan follows `.agent/PLANS.md`. It governs the end-to-end execution
of the Geo-Causal Measurement & Econometrics Benchmark (AWARE-Geo). It must be
updated whenever code, scientific state, or evidence changes.

## Purpose / Big Picture

Build and evaluate an assumption-aware measurement decision system for
geographic causal inference. The primary scientific domain is randomized geo
experimentation (Track A); a separate secondary domain covers observational
staggered panels (Track B). AWARE-Geo uses only pre-treatment diagnostics,
placebo evidence, and design information to gate, select, blend, or reject
estimators before protected post-treatment outcomes are inspected. It is not a
claim of a universal treatment-effect estimator.

Success is an auditable terminal state for mandatory phases E00--E20: qualified
environment and upstream implementations, authoritative data manifests,
known-truth simulation, predeclared development and freeze, one-way frozen E14
evaluation, separate observational and external evidence, canonical Parquet
results, reports, claim ledger, and reproducibility audit. E21 is optional.

only when its expected schemas and hashes verify, warnings/failures are retained,

master prompt, a cleanly identified local commit, and annotated tag. E14

acceptance requires valid terminal states for E00--E20, canonical Parquet
evidence, all requested reports/figures, a reproducible claim ledger, passing
engineering gates, and one scientifically justified final decision.

## Idempotence and Recovery

All writes of canonical artifacts use temporary sibling files followed by
atomic replacement. Each stage verifies receipts and content hashes before
reuse. A failed/incomplete stage may resume from its last verified checkpoint;
immutable completed stages are not recomputed silently. Acquisition pins source
identity and detects upstream drift. Seeds derive from stored world identities,
not iteration order. GPU batches persist summaries and seeds, not full tensors.

No operation may overwrite a freeze manifest, protected world set, canonical
E14 result, or truth-access ledger. Infrastructure-only recovery under a frozen
protocol must prove unchanged inputs, code semantics, and protected evidence.
A semantic defect after freeze creates a new protocol identity; the old attempt
remains preserved and classified.
